Know your way around

Small tools for the whole journey.

From a quick fare check to a saved trip summary, HARI helps you make sense of your commute.

Estimate your fare

Choose your origin, destination, vehicle and tariff preset. Apply supported passenger discounts and review the estimate.

Record your ride

Start GPS metering for distance, time and a running fare estimate. Finish the ride to keep its summary.

Take the map offline

Browse the Philippines and use supported road routing after the detailed map and routing packs are ready.

Keep your presets

Create a named fare preset for the vehicle and rates you use. Review or edit it whenever your local rates change.

Revisit your trips

View saved routes, distances and fare summaries. Your Home screen shows today’s total and your recorded activity.

Ask HARI for help

Get English or Filipino guidance on fares, places, presets and app controls, with optional online source searches.

Your first biyahe

Wake HARI.
Make yourself at home.

First-time setup introduces your commute area and fare preferences. Then a short tour highlights the real controls in the app.

Give your map some room.
The detailed map and routing data make HARI a large download—about 1 GB. Use Wi-Fi for installation and keep extra free space for the extracted offline map. Setup must finish before the main app opens.

  1. A sleepy hello

    Tap to wake HARI. When HARI falls asleep again, tap a second time to continue the wake-up, stretch and peek sequence.

  2. Set up your commute

    Enter an optional display name, select your area and review a fare preset. GPS permission enables ride recording; manual fare estimates can be used without it.

  3. Let the offline map finish

    Keep the app open while it prepares the detailed map. If setup needs attention, check storage and connectivity, then use Retry.

  4. Follow the on-screen tour

    Tap to continue through Start Trip, Quick Fare, the + preset button, History, Chat and Settings. Replay it from Settings → Show me around.

  5. Plan or ride

    Use Quick Fare before you travel. Use Start Trip while riding, then finish the trip to save its summary.

How do I add a preset?

Tap the + button in the bottom bar. Add a recognisable name, choose the vehicle and fare method, and enter the applicable rates. Review and save. Open Settings → Manage Presets to edit or select a preset later.

Before you head out

A few useful answers.

More help is available through HARI’s chat and the replayable in-app tour.

Does HARI work without internet?

After setup, core fare tools, saved presets, trip history, GPS metering and the installed map work offline. Supported road routing needs its routing data. Online source checks, web searches and ads need a connection.

Are the displayed fares official or guaranteed?

They are estimates based on configured presets or available source evidence. Check the selected formula, date and source status. Confirm the fare with the operator or posted fare matrix before riding. HARI is independent of transport operators and government agencies.

Why does HARI ask me to confirm a location?

Several places share a name. Check the barangay, city or municipality and province before choosing. A more specific location helps avoid a route to the wrong place.

What if HARI does not know the answer?

HARI should say when it cannot answer reliably or needs more details. Add a clearer route, vehicle or province, check source evidence, or use the app’s fare tools directly.

Where are my trips and chats stored?

Your profile, trips, presets and chat records are stored on your device. HARI does not require an account. Optional online searches and Android advertising involve third-party services; see the Privacy Policy for details and privacy choices.

How do I delete my saved information?

Open Settings → App Memory & Storage. Choose Clear Trips, Clear Chats & AI Memory, or Delete All Data. Review the confirmation carefully. Local records cannot be restored after deletion unless you have kept a separate copy.

How do ads work?

Android builds include Google advertising with applicable consent and privacy controls. Ads are excluded from active rides, chat and the setup tour. Where available, open Settings → Ad privacy options to review your choices.

Where can I download HARI?
