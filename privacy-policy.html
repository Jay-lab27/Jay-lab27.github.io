HARI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026  |  App Version: 1.0.0

HARI is an independent, offline-first Philippine commute assistant. Your profile, trip history, GPS rides, custom fare presets, and chat history are stored locally on your device. No user accounts or remote tracking servers are used. General web search is OFF by default. Public tariff evidence checks are available at every age; daily preset checks are enabled by default when HARI opens online and can be disabled in Tariff Updates.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Age group: A locally stored age group, or an unspecified-age choice. The entered age number is not retained or transmitted.
  • Optional Profile Name: Your chosen display name (you may also continue as a guest).
  • Default Commute Area: Region, province, city/municipality, and optional barangay you select.
  • Discount Eligibility Preference: Whether you toggle the 20% Student, Senior Citizen, or PWD discount calculation.
  • Trip History: Up to 150 saved rides, including route names, distances, fares, timestamps, up to 120 sampled GPS route points per trip, and any optional vehicle plate/body number you enter.
  • Local Chat & AI Memory: Up to 30 chat sessions (up to 100 messages each) and optional saved commute notes (such as Home/Work locations or preferred vehicles). HARI blocks saving passwords, PINs, and payment card numbers in AI memory.

2. Local Storage & Security

  • Android (Android 10+ / API 29+): Local preferences, trip histories, chat records, and AI memory are encrypted at rest using Android Keystore AES-256-GCM encryption and excluded from Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfers.
  • iOS: Local preferences, trip histories, chat records, and AI memory are stored in iOS UserDefaults (protected by iOS system Data Protection when your device is locked), and offline map files are excluded from iCloud Backup.
  • Network Transport: Optional online requests use HTTPS encryption in transit.

3. GPS Location & Device Permissions

  • Location (Precise / Approximate): Used strictly on-device to center the map, estimate distances, and record active rides you start. HARI does not upload your GPS coordinates to any server.
  • Active Ride Background Tracking: While you have an active ride running, Android uses a Location Foreground Service with an ongoing notification, and iOS uses background location updates with the system location indicator so distance metering continues if your screen locks. Tracking stops when you end the trip.
  • Notifications (Android 13+): Requested only to display the ongoing fare meter notification during an active trip. HARI does not send promotional push notifications.
  • Phone Dialer, Email & Share: Opening emergency hotlines, tapping Feedback to message the developer via your email app, or sharing a trip summary uses your device's standard dialer, email app, or share sheet only when you tap those actions. HARI never reads your contacts or emails. Trip sharing and copying trip summaries for external use are available only to adult profiles. An adult who chooses Share may send trip details and a location snapshot to the app they select.

4. Local AI Companion & Optional Online Requests

HARI's chat companion runs locally on your device using rule-based logic and bundled Philippine Standard Geographic Code (PSGC) data.

  • Optional Online Search (OFF by default): Adult profiles may enable the "Search" chip in Chat. Query text is sent over HTTPS to public search providers, including Bing (bing.com), DuckDuckGo (html/lite/api.duckduckgo.com) or Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org), as applicable. Adult tariff-source discovery may use search providers only when this separate web-search setting is enabled. Please avoid entering sensitive personal details when online search is turned on.
  • Public Tariff Source Checks (available at every age): Daily preset evidence checks run once per day when HARI opens online. You may turn them off in Tariff Updates; separately scheduled background source monitoring is OFF by default. Routine daily and manual checks download a signed catalog from GitHub Pages (jay-lab27.github.io). GitHub receives normal connection data, including the device IP address. Original fare documents are reviewed before catalog publication. Separate source research can fetch public government or LGU websites; broader search-provider discovery remains adult-only and requires the web-search option. No personal profile, entered age, GPS coordinates, trips or chats are attached to catalog checks.
  • Offline Map Delivery (Android): On Google Play installations, offline map and routing packs are delivered via Google Play Asset Delivery.

5. Third-Party SDKs, Ads & Analytics

After the optional age screen and applicable consent checks, age-12-and-older profiles and profiles that skip age entry may be eligible for ads. Age-12 and unspecified-age profiles receive only a single child-directed Home native placement using the Families-certified Google Mobile Ads SDK; ages 13–17 receive restricted, non-personalized requests. Profiles explicitly entered as under 12 receive no ads. Skipping does not assign an age; unknown-age ad requests use child-directed G-rated treatment with personalization disabled and no advertising ID. Public tariff refresh is available at every age and is separate from advertising and general web search.

Android builds include Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). When advertising is enabled, Google may process IP addresses (which can imply approximate location), advertising/device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to applicable privacy choices. HARI does not provide GPS coordinates to the advertising SDK; Google may infer approximate location from the IP address. HARI does not attach profile details, GPS coordinates, trip endpoints/history, saved locations, AI conversations or payment information to advertising or analytics requests.

Consent information is checked before requesting ads. Where required, review your choices in Settings → Ad privacy options. Ad availability follows UMP's permitted-request state, the build configuration and applicable restrictions; a permitted ad request does not mean you agreed to personalized ads. Core functions remain usable offline; there are no ads in active trips, chat or the setup tour. iOS contains no advertising SDK.

Aggregate ad counters are stored locally for diagnostics. No separate behavioral analytics or crash-reporting service is added; Google's ad SDK performs its own measurement. Read Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partners. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal trip data. Offline maps and road routing use OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0) rendered locally via MapLibre and GraphHopper, with Google Noto Sans map glyphs (OFL-1.1).

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device only for as long as you keep it. You can delete data at any time inside the app:

  • Clear trip history: Settings → App Memory & Storage → Clear Trips.
  • Clear chats & AI memory: Settings → App Memory & Storage → Clear Chats & AI Memory.
  • Delete all personal data: Settings → App Memory & Storage → Delete All Data.
  • Uninstalling HARI also permanently removes all locally stored app data from your device.

7. Your Privacy Rights (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 — RA 10173)

Under Republic Act No. 10173 and applicable laws, you have the right to be informed, to access, to rectify/correct, to object to or withdraw consent (including disabling location permissions or optional online search at any time), to erasure or blocking of personal data, to data portability, to damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.

8. Children's Privacy

HARI supports a mixed audience, including children. A neutral age screen stores only an age group on the device, or an unspecified-age choice. Only a local advertising category (under 12, age 12, ages 13–17, adult, or unspecified) is retained; the entry is not transmitted. Profiles explicitly entered as under 12 receive no advertising requests. Age-12 and unspecified-age profiles receive only one child-directed, non-personalized Home placement with G-rated treatment and no advertising ID. Unknown age stays unspecified. Teen profiles receive restricted, non-personalized advertising, subject to the applicable consent checks. General web/chat search and external trip sharing remain adult-only. Public tariff-source checks are available regardless of age; official source websites receive the device IP address while serving public documents. Broad search-provider discovery is used only when the separate adult web-search option is enabled. HARI does not send the age category, entered age, GPS coordinates, trip history or chat messages with these checks. Offline fare calculations, local maps, local trip records and the on-device assistant remain available. GPS information used for a user-started trip stays on the device. Children should use HARI with a trusted adult's guidance. Parents or guardians may review or erase locally saved data via Settings → Delete All Data.
